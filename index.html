The Story of ORO Pizza

More than just the Italian word for gold—to us, ORO stands for Our Relationship with Others.

Taking its name from the Italian word for "gold," ORO Pizza was born out of a passion to bring authentic Italian craftsmanship to the heart of Egypt. What started as a simple love for true Neapolitan dough quickly grew into a beloved local destination—from Giza to 6th of October. Our philosophy has always been simple: great pizza takes time, fresh ingredients matter, and every single slice should feel like a golden experience worth sharing.

At ORO, we blend classic Italian baking traditions with bold, crowd-pleasing Egyptian favorites. Whether you are craving a timeless Margherita, a rich four-cheese blend, or our signature Crispy Chicken Ranch and Kiri Pastrami creations, every pizza is handcrafted and baked to crispy, airy perfection. Whether it's a casual night out or a family gathering, we're here to serve unforgettable flavors straight from our oven to your table.
